# Jurisdictional Recession in Practice

*Robodebt as a development case for the configuration-first diagnostic protocol*

**Worked case · revised July 2026**  
Provisional analysis pending page-level citation verification against the Royal Commission record

## 1. Purpose, status, and question

This document is the development-case application of the revised configuration-first method. Jurisdictional recession enters as a provisional diagnostic hypothesis, not a result to be confirmed. The case asks whether Robodebt exhibits a specific failure: authoritative effects continued to be produced through relations that formal or practical correction could not adequately reach. It does not claim to explain the scheme in its entirety or replace legal, administrative, political, or misconduct-based accounts.

The analysis has four purposes. It tests whether the method identifies authority-producing relations more precisely than the loose phrase ‘production logic’; reconstructs how corrective jurisdiction was constituted around those relations; distinguishes recession from ordinary error, opacity, and traversable responsibility; and derives provisional design conditions that could keep consequential responses within effective corrective reach.

| **PROVISIONAL RESULT:** The evidence presently supports a jurisdictionally receded finding, principally through expansion, asymmetrical formation, and unequal transmission rather than simple relocation. Authority production extended across temporal apportionment, evidentiary sufficiency, automated routing, and recovery. Contrary responses existed, but early correction remained concentrated on individual debt outputs and did not consistently acquire the visibility, meaning, and force required to change the shared production relation. Confidence varies by sub-claim; the ledger identifies what still requires pinpoint verification. |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## 2. Case boundary and primary-source base

The principal case boundary is the Income Compliance Program commonly called Robodebt, from its commencement in 2015 until the abandonment of reliance on averaged ATO income data in November 2019. Later inquiries, refunds, settlements, integrity findings, and compensation mechanisms are treated as the repair history of that bounded configuration, not as though they were part of its original operation.

Services Australia describes Robodebts as debts raised between July 2015 and November 2019 using averaged Australian Taxation Office income information. The Royal Commission reported in July 2023. The 2017 and 2019 Commonwealth Ombudsman reports examined the online compliance system. In March 2026, the National Anti-Corruption Commission found serious corrupt conduct by two of six referred individuals and no corrupt conduct by the remaining four under its statutory test. In June 2026, the Federal Court approved an additional class-action settlement. A separate 2026 Income Apportionment Resolution Scheme also shows that temporal apportionment problems extend beyond the program boundary; it is treated as contextual evidence, not silently folded into the Robodebt case.

This draft relies on the official source set listed in Section 17. Detailed propositions still require page-level pinning to the Royal Commission volumes and relevant judgments. Where the evidence currently supports only a plausible analytic interpretation, the text marks it as a hypothesis rather than a finding.

## 3. The diagnostic object

### 3.1 What JR is being asked to explain

The explanatory target is not that Robodebt was unlawful, inaccurate, harmful, automated, or badly administered. Those facts can be explained through established legal and administrative accounts. The narrower question is why authoritative debt effects persisted despite the existence of internal review, merits review, an Ombudsman investigation, legal expertise, complaints, and institutional awareness of difficulties. JR is tested as an explanation of failed corrective reach and persistence.

### 3.2 Outcome vocabulary

- Aligned: authority production and effective corrective jurisdiction are sufficiently coextensive for the relation being examined.

- Diffuse but traversable: responsibility is distributed, but challenge and repair can still reach the material authority-producing relations.

- Jurisdictionally receded: authority-producing relations materially exceed epistemic, procedural, operational, attributive, or temporal corrective reach, producing the specified channel/load/fault misalignment.

The case is not forced into one status at every scale. A local review episode may be aligned even if the program-level configuration is receded. These contrasts are part of the evidence rather than exceptions to be removed.

### 3.3 Comparative conceptual operation

The case operationalises a compact Chinese-derived configurational grammar without treating cultural origin as evidence. 形 directs reconstruction of what had already taken form; 位／连 traces how capacity changed with position and connection; 势／向 reads the direction and propensity produced by the arrangement; 机／权 locates relations with disproportionate leverage; and 化／变 traces what actually changed between successive configurations. The democratic purpose is to let evidence from recipient, frontline, review, oversight, and other materially affected positions alter the represented configuration without requiring those positions to become institutional arrangers. The Robodebt record must determine whether these operations add analytic value.

## 4. Dispositional arrangement

The configuration did not mechanically determine every debt or response. It disposed some objects and routes toward institutional action more strongly than others. Four mutually conditioning dimensions reconstruct that propensity.

### 4.1 Knowledge constitution

Annual ATO income data was apportioned across shorter social-security periods when more specific evidence was absent or not supplied. The crucial issue was not merely that an average could be inaccurate. It was that the apportioned figure acquired a role in establishing and quantifying a debt despite the mismatch between annual data and the shorter periods on which entitlement depended. The authority of the debt therefore rested partly on a temporal transformation and partly on the evidentiary rule that permitted that transformation to carry institutional force.

The Royal Commission record concerning internal legal advice is relevant here because it may show that doubts about the lawful evidentiary role of averaging were available during formation. The stronger claim that advice was simply ‘excluded at formation’ will be retained only if the page-level chronology supports that precise description.

### 4.2 Institutional routing

The system primarily routed contestation through the person’s individual income record: confirm, correct, supply evidence, request review, or appeal a debt. This route could correct some outputs, but it did not automatically convert repeated output disputes into a general challenge to the authority of income apportionment itself. Program-level legality, evidentiary sufficiency, user burden, and individual calculation were therefore not equally available as objects of correction.

### 4.3 Operational dominance

Income apportionment fitted the data already held and supported large-scale compliance activity. Its operational advantages included repeatability, administrative convenience, throughput, integration with digital communication, and compatibility with fiscal expectations. These advantages help explain dominance but do not by themselves prove deliberate suppression or illegality. The diagnostic question is whether institutional fit enabled the transformed figure to keep authorising action after contrary signals arose.

### 4.4 Temporal disposition

- Windowing and apportionment converted annual earnings into shorter-period income and could erase variation within the year.

- Look-back periods increased the distance between the alleged overpayment and the person’s need to reconstruct specific earnings evidence.

- Response periods and consequences of non-response gave institutional and personal time unequal operational effects.

- Individual correction could occur on a different timescale from program-level recognition, allowing local repair without immediate general change.

The defensible temporal finding is that time operations helped constitute the actionable debt object and the practical burden of contesting it. Claims that a particular reset mechanism was deliberately designed to suppress precedent require separate proof and are not treated as established merely because individual debts were revised or reduced.

## 5. Authority production

The unit of analysis is an authority-producing relation: a documented relation without which the relevant object would not have acquired the same capacity to authorise institutional action. The current reconstruction identifies seven candidate relations.

| **Authority-producing relation**      | **Institutional effect**                                                                                                          | **Evidence status**                                                                                                           |
|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Action-conditioned evidentiary demand | A proposed compliance or debt action required a case-level object capable of authorising it.                                      | Analytic hypothesis; the historical sequence and alternatives require decision-record verification.                           |
| Annual-to-period apportionment        | Created a period-specific representation from annual ATO data.                                                                    | Secure at program level; implementation detail requires citation pinning.                                                     |
| Evidentiary-authorisation hinge       | Allowed the apportioned representation to be admitted as sufficient evidence and participate in establishing or quantifying debt. | Central legal issue; secure in broad form, but the permissions, actors, and variations constituting the hinge must be mapped. |
| Recipient-response routing            | Made the person’s confirmation, correction, evidence, or non-response part of the route by which the object hardened.             | Supported by Ombudsman and Commission materials; versions over time must be distinguished.                                    |
| Program-scale operationalisation      | Embedded the relation in repeatable digital and administrative processes.                                                         | Secure as general structure; avoid assuming full automation at every stage.                                                   |
| Recovery and enforcement              | Converted the debt object into demands, repayment, offsets, fees, or other consequences.                                          | Secure generally; individual pathways varied.                                                                                 |

No single relation is asserted to be the sole cause of Robodebt. Their conjunction matters: apportionment without evidentiary force would remain a rough estimate; evidentiary force without operational routing would not produce program-scale effects; review without operational capacity to alter the relation might correct outputs while leaving the configuration intact.

The formation hypothesis runs backwards from the scheme’s later administrative presentation. A debt object may appear to emerge from evidence and then trigger action. The research instead asks whether a preferred action first created an evidentiary demand, for which annual data and apportionment supplied a convenient formal bridge. This locates a possible hinge that manufactured actionable authority; it does not establish why annual averaging was selected, or that selection was deliberate wrongdoing, without documentary evidence.

### 5.1 Annual averaging as an authorised marker

Annual income data was not intrinsically false. The design question is whether the conditions under which it could validly indicate income in a particular social-security period were preserved when the marker crossed temporal and institutional contexts. The case will therefore record the source period, target period, variability assumptions, defeating evidence, revalidation route, and the permission that allowed the marker to authorise action. The image in 《吕氏春秋·察今》 of a fixed marker remaining after the river has moved is used only as a contemporary heuristic for this test, not as historical evidence about Robodebt.

### 5.2 Configurational revision of the hinge

|------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Formation and direction      | Annual data, apportionment and workflow became a scalable route to coordinated compliance action.   | What formed capacities, constraints and momentum made this bridge attractive and repeatable?                                    |

## 6. Jurisdiction production

Jurisdiction is analysed here as effective reach around the authority-producing configuration, not only formal legal competence. The question is what could be known, admitted, changed, attributed, and repaired in time.

| **Reachability** | **What existed**                                                                                                      | **Observed limitation**                                                                                                                                                                                                     | **Provisional assessment**                                               |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Epistemic        | Notices, internal records, complaints, reviews, legal advice, oversight inquiries.                                    | Recipients could see a claimed discrepancy or debt without necessarily seeing how apportionment and evidentiary sufficiency jointly produced it; information available to oversight could also be incomplete or misleading. | Partial reach; stronger internally than from the recipient position.     |
| Procedural       | Internal review, Administrative Appeals Tribunal review, Ombudsman, courts and later inquiries.                       | Most routine routes were oriented to individual outcomes; a successful individual challenge did not necessarily compel a general answer about the program relation.                                                         | Output reach existed; production-layer reach was uneven.                 |
| Operational      | Officials could revise debts; government could change policy; courts and later processes could constrain operation.   | Many reviewing sites could alter an individual outcome more readily than suspend or redesign the authority-producing relation.                                                                                              | Material asymmetry between local and program-level alteration.           |
| Attributive      | Administrative, ministerial, legal, parliamentary, integrity and employment-accountability structures.                | Later processes applied different legal tests and attached responsibility to some conduct without necessarily locating one bearer for the full configuration.                                                               | Distributed; not equivalent to absence of all responsibility.            |
| Temporal         | Immediate contacts and reviews; later litigation, refunds, Royal Commission, settlements and integrity investigation. | Program-level correction and compensation arrived after debts and burdens had already accumulated; some consequences were not fully reversible.                                                                             | Corrective reach increased over time but was late relative to operation. |

This matrix prevents the binary claim that ‘review existed’ or ‘review was impossible.’ Robodebt contained real correction channels. The relevant finding is that their scope, object, power, and timing did not initially match the scope of authority production.

## 7. Recession dynamics and formation pattern

### 7.1 Authority differentiation

The program’s legally visible decision points did not exhaust the production of authority. The debt’s operative force depended on relations among data, temporal transformation, evidentiary assumptions, communication, recipient response, administrative processing, and recovery. This is primarily an expansion finding: authority production occupied more relations than the routine review object represented.

### 7.2 Contestability blockage

Recipients could contest calculations and sometimes obtain revision. The blockage was therefore not total. It arose where the available route individualised the dispute while the disputed authority-producing relation operated across cases. Tribunal and legal-review histories must be mapped carefully to determine when production-layer objections became procedurally and operationally effective.

### 7.3 Load displacement

Recipients carried financial, evidentiary, administrative, and psychological burdens while having little control over the underlying data transformation or program design. Staff also carried operational and ethical burdens, but their institutional position was not equivalent to that of recipients. The Royal Commission’s detailed account of effects on individuals supplies the primary evidence for realised load.

### 7.4 Stabilisation

Plausible stabilising relations include fiscal commitments, program throughput, fraud and compliance framing, institutional reliance on existing data, fragmented complaints, unequal information, and the ability to resolve some cases locally without immediately answering the general question. Three claims require separate evidence: local correction could reduce pressure for general change; misleading information impaired an oversight channel in at least one documented instance; and deliberate system-wide threshold suppression remains a hypothesis requiring pattern evidence.

### 7.4.1 Recursive momentum

Momentum did not necessarily remain constant between major decisions. Each new data cycle, operational adjustment, individual correction, policy clarification, review response, or technical revision could interrupt or redirect the scheme, but could also add investment, precedent, data, dependency, and apparent responsiveness while preserving the goal, evidentiary framing, and authorisation hinge. The case will therefore test updates individually: an update counts as corrective only to the extent that it changes the relevant authority-producing relation, not merely because it changes selected outputs.

### 7.4.2 Unequal transmission of change

A complaint, correction, tribunal outcome, frontline workaround, warning, or oversight finding did not automatically become a change in the operative configuration. To do so, it had to remain visible, preserve its systemic meaning, travel across the necessary relations, and acquire alteration force. The working hypothesis is that signals aligned with program momentum—throughput, fiscal expectation, reliance on available data, and continuation of established workflow—could be repeated, aggregated, authorised, and accelerated. Contrary signals could be separated into individual cases, delayed, recoded as user error or implementation difficulty, or absorbed as evidence that review existed. The case will test this transmission claim rather than infer it from persistence alone.

For each significant response, the reconstruction will record its source, timing, classification, recipients, subsequent route, aggregation or fragmentation, decision consequence, and evidentiary status. This allows the case to distinguish three outcomes: direction reinforced; response retained as consequential load or residue without configurational force; and genuine reconfiguration of the authority-producing relation.

### 7.4.3 Masterton–Amato and the non-absorbable challenge

The Victoria Legal Aid litigation sequence offers a focused comparison of how an individual challenge can lose or retain configurational force. In the Masterton matter, the debt was reduced to zero and the Commonwealth argued that no live legal issue remained. The later Amato test case continued after the asserted debt was substantially reduced; the Commonwealth subsequently conceded that central uses of averaging, the penalty and tax-refund seizure in that case were unlawful, and the Federal Court made consent orders.

The research will reconstruct the different arrangements around these matters: the legal object carried by the claim, evidence, remedy, affected person, sustained representation, court jurisdiction, departmental response, public attention and connection to other litigation. Non-absorbable challenge is a provisional description of a challenge whose object and connections preserve its capacity to test the common production relation after local correction. The episode will be tested as a possible transition from warning to configurational force, with historical causation and the contribution of each relation established from the record.

### 7.5 Repair limitation

Repair was substantial but staged. Ending reliance on averaged data, reducing debts to zero, refunds, litigation settlements, the Royal Commission, later integrity findings, and newer compensation mechanisms reached different objects under different legal and administrative tests. It would be inaccurate to say that repair never reached production: the use of averaged ATO data to raise Robodebts ended, and later processes named structural failures. The narrower finding is that early correction often reached individual outcomes more readily than the authority-producing relations, while later repair arrived after accumulated and partly irreversible load.

### 7.6 Formation pattern

- Expansion is the principal pattern: the production of authoritative debt effects extended across more relations than routine corrective jurisdiction could reach.

- Asymmetrical formation is also supported: the program was scaled with strong production capacity while equivalent production-layer contestability and monitoring were not demonstrated at the same scope.

- Relocation is secondary and relation-specific: some effective authority may have shifted from individual officer assessment toward data transformation and system settings, but the case does not require a fictional earlier state in which all authority sat at one reviewable point.

### 7.7 Dynamic agency and conduct

Systemic momentum and deliberate wrongdoing are not competing complete explanations. Conduct can enter at particular transitions and change as warnings accumulate. The case will therefore reconstruct a dynamic register rather than place the whole scheme at one point on a simple intent-to-error spectrum.

| **Transition**                         | **Agency-and-conduct question**                                                                                                                   | **Evidence required**                                                                              |
|----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Problem framing and goal selection     | Whose purposes made some explanations and actions more attractive than alternatives?                                                              | Decision records, options, sponsors, stated reasons, political and fiscal commitments.             |
| Formal bridge and authorisation        | Who approved the operation and its evidentiary status; what uncertainty and alternatives were known?                                              | Design records, legal and policy advice, approvals, specifications, evidentiary rules.             |
| Operation and reinforcement            | Which warnings, reversals, complaints, or contrary decisions became known, and to whom?                                                           | Briefings, correspondence, escalation records, review outcomes, meeting records.                   |
| Continuation after knowledge threshold | Did conduct inherit, negligently maintain, interpret toward a preferred result, recklessly defend, conceal, or knowingly exploit the arrangement? | Actor-specific knowledge, authority, alternatives, conduct, causal effect, and defeating evidence. |

No conduct category is assigned by configuration alone. The map explains differential enablement and momentum; historical selection requires decision evidence; findings of knowledge, intention, concealment, or exploitation require actor-specific proof.

Outcome valence is also kept separate from premise and process validity. A debt could have been correctly identified in a particular case even if the starting frame or evidentiary route was underjustified; a legitimate compliance concern could also produce harmful effects through an invalid process. Positive, negative, or mixed outcomes therefore cannot retrospectively validate or invalidate every relation that preceded them.

## 8. Channel, realised load, and corrective responsibility

The final criterion follows three different traces rather than treating them as interchangeable positions.

- Channel: ATO income data, apportionment, evidentiary treatment, digital contact, recipient response, administrative processing, and recovery carried authorisation and effects.

- Realised load: recipients carried debt demands, evidentiary work, repayment or recovery consequences, uncertainty, stigma, and documented distress; some staff carried secondary operational and ethical burdens.

- Fault-bearing or corrective responsibility: responsibility was tested through administrative review, courts, government, the Royal Commission, employment processes, the NACC, and settlements, each with different jurisdiction, evidentiary standards, remedies, and objects.

The misalignment is not that no responsibility ever attached. It is that those carrying the primary load lacked effective control over the authority-producing channel, while early corrective routes were narrower than that channel. Later processes increased epistemic, operational, attributive, and compensatory reach, but did so after years of operation and under fragmented mandates.

| **DIAGNOSTIC CRITERION:** The program-level finding is jurisdictionally receded if page-level evidence confirms that production-layer challenge was materially less reachable than output challenge, that this asymmetry enabled continued authoritative effects, and that the realised load was thereby displaced onto actors unable to control the relevant relations. The finding weakens if the record shows that early review routinely translated individual challenges into effective program-level correction. |

### 8.1 Field responses and instrument capture

Recipient corrections, non-response, requests for assistance, complaints, workarounds, delay, frontline compensatory labour, repeated exceptions, review reversals, and residual harm are candidate field responses. They are not automatically proof of one failure or of reconfiguration, but neither should they be discarded as noise or individual noncompliance before testing what mismatch they disclose. The evidence review will ask which signals were aggregated or fragmented, who could see them, whether they retained their systemic meaning, how they were classified, and what subsequent relation they could alter.

Safeguards also require an operative test. Individual review, oversight, reporting, legal expertise, and later corrective updates may constrain a program, but they may also be absorbed as evidence of responsiveness while the authorisation hinge remains intact. The case will therefore distinguish formal presence from production reach: did the instrument change the shared rule, or help the arrangement continue by resolving local pressure?

## 9. Counterinstances and negative findings

A rigorous case must record where jurisdiction did not recede.

- Individual review could sometimes correct or remove a debt. At that scale, corrective jurisdiction could be aligned or traversable even while the program relation persisted.

- Tribunal review created a route through which evidentiary insufficiency could be recognised. The unresolved question is how effectively those decisions travelled back into program operation.

- The 2019 legal and governmental change reached the central authority-producing relation by ending reliance on averaged ATO data for Robodebts. This is a later operational-reach success, not evidence that recession was permanent.

- The Royal Commission created strong epistemic reach and proposed institutional reform; settlements and refunds created compensatory reach; the NACC created attributive findings under a narrower corrupt-conduct jurisdiction.

These counterinstances refine the diagnosis. JR describes a historically situated asymmetry, not an eternal property of an institution. Correction can regain jurisdiction, but late reach does not erase loads already realised or prove that all relevant relations became co-reachable.

JR is warranted only if it adds an evidenced relation that these rivals do not already explain: the mismatch between the scope of operative authority and the effective scope of correction. It should coexist with rival accounts where they explain different parts of the case.

The same chronology may move across these categories. An arrangement can begin from a legitimate concern, acquire direction through selective framing, and later be negligently, recklessly, deceptively, or knowingly maintained by particular actors. System explanation must not erase agency; misconduct findings must not make the wider authority-producing configuration disappear.

## 11. Diagnostic result

| **RESULT: JURISDICTIONALLY RECEDED — PROVISIONAL.** At program level, authority production expanded across temporal apportionment, evidentiary sufficiency, recipient-response routing, operational scaling, and recovery. Corrective jurisdiction existed but was initially narrower: contrary responses more readily changed individual outputs than the relations authorising outputs across the program. Momentum-aligned changes could reinforce operation while opposing signals lost visibility, systemic meaning, or alteration force. Realised burden accumulated on recipients who could not control those relations. Later correction regained substantial jurisdiction, but after prolonged operation and partly irreversible burden. |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

The result is conditional rather than absolute. The strongest evidence supports the existence of averaged-income Robodebts, recipient burden, program-scale operation, available but uneven review, the 2019 cessation, later refunds and settlements, Royal Commission findings, and the 2026 NACC findings. The more ambitious claims—deliberate threshold suppression, the precise handling and institutional effect of tribunal decisions, and the full 2014–2019 legal-advice chronology—remain subject to page-level verification.

## 12. Provisional anti-recession design conditions

The case supports design requirements, not a complete technical solution. Each principle must be tested against other cases before generalisation.

| **Design condition**            | **Robodebt diagnosis**                                                                                                                    | **Possible future requirement**                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|---------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Jurisdictional co-extension     | Authority scaled across data, rules, workflow and recovery while correction was often output-centred.                                     | Whenever authority production scales or spreads, review competence and operational power must expand across the same relations.                                                                                                                   |
| Production-layer contestability | A person could dispute a debt without necessarily converting the dispute into a challenge to the common evidentiary relation.             | Provide an explicit route for challenging shared models, transformations, categories and evidentiary rules, with trigger conditions for general review.                                                                                           |
| Action–evidence separation      | A formal transformation could bridge the gap between an administratively desired action and adequate case-level evidence.                 | Document the proposed action, the evidence needed to authorise it, uncertainty introduced by any formal bridge, and the permission through which its output becomes actionable.                                                                   |
| Knowledge-threshold escalation  | Repeated warnings can change the character of continued operation even where the originating concern was legitimate.                      | Define actor-specific duties to record, aggregate, escalate, suspend, and independently review the arrangement as contrary evidence and foreseeable load accumulate.                                                                              |
| Control–obligation coupling     | Recipients carried load without control; responsibility was later divided across different mandates.                                      | Positions able to create, maintain, approve, or benefit from an authority-producing relation must inherit defined monitoring, explanation, suspension and repair obligations.                                                                     |
| Temporal reversibility          | Program-level correction followed years of burden; evidence and personal capacity could decay.                                            | Review and escalation must operate within periods in which the object and consequences remain meaningfully reversible.                                                                                                                            |
| Load visibility                 | Individual administrative and psychological work was not equivalent to program throughput metrics.                                        | Monitoring must aggregate correction work, complaints, reversals, non-response, distress indicators and distributional burden as system signals.                                                                                                  |
| Jurisdictional inheritance      | Authority travelled through data sharing, administrative rules, digital systems and recovery relations.                                   | When an authority-producing function is delegated to data, software, contractors or another institution, corrective obligations and access must travel with it.                                                                                   |
| Configurational visibility      | Recipients and some reviewers encountered individual debt objects more readily than the whole arrangement and its direction of operation. | Affected people and corrective bodies must be able to perceive what the arrangement makes easy, cumulative, authoritative and resistant to correction; knowledge from their positions must also be capable of revising the system representation. |

These principles operate at three levels: system design (data, workflow, monitoring and escalation), institutional design (competence, obligation and review), and cybernetic design (whether corrective channels are coextensive with the relations capable of producing authoritative effects).

## 13. Relationship to cybernetics

Robodebt provides an empirical setting for examining the reach of feedback and correction. Recipients responded, officials reviewed, tribunals decided, complaints accumulated, and oversight bodies investigated. The case examines how transmission was conditioned: why some changes were amplified into continued operation while other consequential returns altered outputs yet failed to reconfigure the arrangement producing them.

Dispositional arrangement names the evidence-bounded reconstruction through which program-scale production, individualised review, institutional timing, and burden distribution acquired unequal force. Configurational propensity names the direction generated by that arrangement: debt authority travelled toward action more readily than repeated individual correction travelled toward alteration of the common relation. The operation appears in aligned as well as failing relations, so the comparison tests configuration rather than inferring it only from pathology.

The cybernetic contribution concerns the jurisdiction of feedback over authority production. Feedback can exist and operate locally while lacking the reach to alter the common relation producing authoritative effects. Configurational propensity identifies how the arrangement amplifies some changes and attenuates others.

- Pin every legal-advice claim to the Royal Commission chronology and distinguish advice content, recipient, institutional response, and operational effect.

- Map versions of the program over time so claims about notices, human involvement, evidentiary use, non-response, fees, and review do not collapse distinct periods.

- Test the tribunal-propagation claim against official decisions, publication practices, departmental escalation records, and the Ombudsman’s 2024 work on learning from merits review.

## 16. Scope of the development case

The case provisionally establishes that the protocol can reconstruct authority production and jurisdiction production separately; represent partial reachability; identify a formation pattern; retain counterinstances; compare rival explanations; and derive design requirements. Authority expanded across relations, while the reach of correction changed over time and later recovered in several forms.

The findings are bounded to the Robodebt reconstruction. Further methodological testing will examine reproducibility, and the design experiments will test the practical value of the derived conditions. The case also makes inspectable the different positions occupied by causal contribution, formal competence, control, benefit, misconduct and capacity for repair.

Robodebt is also evidentially unusual: realised effects, inquiries, judgments, testimony, and later actor-specific findings permit retrospective reconstruction. A new or emerging case will contain less evidence of actual effect and must not be completed by analogy. The method should instead identify what remains observable, inferred, anticipated, unknown, or defeated; what monitoring could reveal; whose position is missing; and what intervention remains possible before the configuration acquires comparable momentum. Such a result is a prospective jurisdictional hypothesis, not a finding that the new case is another Robodebt.

The case also provides a reflexive test of epistemic and positional asymmetry. If Chinese conceptual resources are acknowledged only as cultural background while English categories control what counts as method and evidence, the research would reproduce the exclusion it seeks to diagnose. Likewise, if the method merely gives recipients access to an institutionally authored account while retaining the institution as its implied knower, it would distribute information without changing whose knowledge can revise the configuration. The concepts are therefore tested bidirectionally: what analytic operation they generate, what English operationalisation preserves or loses, what position each representation presumes, and whether evidence from non-controlling positions changes the map and subsequent inquiry. This gives the work a bounded decolonial significance without turning the case into a comprehensive study of coloniality.

## 18. Provisional conclusion

Robodebt is provisionally diagnosed as jurisdictionally receded because its capacity to produce authoritative debt effects became broader than the early practical jurisdiction of correction. The central pattern was not the disappearance of review and not simply the movement of a decision upstream. It was the expansion and asymmetrical formation of authority across data transformation, evidentiary treatment, workflow, recipient response, and recovery, while correction was more readily organised around individual outputs.

The case also shows that recession can change over time. Later legal, administrative, compensatory, inquiry, and integrity processes regained different forms of reach. Their arrival demonstrates that jurisdiction is produced and can be redesigned; their delay demonstrates why the timing, scope, and object of correction matter. These findings connect the diagnosis to the design of prospective oversight.
