# Reconfiguring Corrective Jurisdiction in Algorithmically Mediated Systems

*Prospective configurational oversight developed through the Robodebt case*

**Short PhD Research Proposal**  

## Project summary

This research investigates how socio-technical arrangements can retain and expand their capacity to produce authoritative effects while the practical reach of challenge, correction and repair becomes comparatively restricted. I call this condition **jurisdictional recession**.

Using the Australian Robodebt scheme as its foundational case, the project will develop **Configurational Jurisdiction Inquiry**: a visual and participatory method for investigating how authority, burden, information and corrective possibility become differently visible and actionable across affected, operational, technical, managerial, legal and oversight positions.

The design objective is to help investigators recognise how an arrangement is acquiring authority and momentum, identify where corrective signals are being weakened or absorbed, and locate interventions capable of changing the configuration before important consequences become difficult or impossible to reverse.

## 1. Research problem

Robodebt was not produced by an algorithm acting alone. Fiscal and political direction, inherited administrative arrangements, annual income data, averaging, evidentiary practices, automated processes, digital communication, recipient burdens, institutional fragmentation and delayed correction became connected in a way that produced debts with practical authority.

Warnings and challenges existed, but their existence did not guarantee correction. Many responses could alter an individual debt or local procedure without reaching the common relations producing similar effects. Information about harm could be fragmented into individual cases, translated into administratively acceptable categories, delayed across institutional boundaries or returned to positions without sufficient power to change the arrangement.

Jurisdictional recession describes the resulting asymmetry:

> Authority-producing relations become more capable of acting than available arrangements of challenge, review and repair are capable of observing, reaching or changing them.

This is not identical to opacity, procedural failure or distributed responsibility. A system may be visible yet difficult to alter. Review may exist but arrive after preventable consequences have become irreversible. Responsibility may be distributed while corrective powers remain attached to narrow decisions rather than the wider configuration producing them.

The project therefore treats accountability as a problem of maintaining **corrective reach** within a changing socio-technical configuration.

## 2. Research question

> **How can inquiry across differently situated positions make an authority-producing configuration revisable and preserve corrective capacity as that configuration forms and changes under conditions of partial information?**

The research will investigate:

1. How did institutional disposition, political pressure, evidentiary demand, formal operations and administrative action become connected in Robodebt?
2. At which transitions did constructed information acquire sufficient authority to reorganise action and shift burdens?
3. Why did some changes travel with the configuration’s momentum while corrective signals became weak, local or invisible?
4. How can investigators identify stress points, power vulnerabilities and changes in reversibility before complete evidence is available?
5. Which interventions could return an observed effect to the configuration strongly enough to alter its subsequent movement?

## 3. Conceptual approach

The unit of analysis is the **successive configuration** through which heterogeneous elements acquire force together over time. This includes people, institutions, purposes, rules, data, algorithms, evidentiary permissions, workflows, political conditions, public narratives, burdens and corrective mechanisms.

A configuration is not assumed to update merely because resistance or harm occurs. A contrary signal may be too fragmented, delayed or opposed to accumulated momentum to change subsequent action. The research follows whether a signal is:

1. perceived;
2. retained as meaningful;
3. translated into an actionable form;
4. carried to a position with alteration power; and
5. returned to the relations producing the problem.

The approach does not remove agency or intention. Ordinary error, incentive-following, negligence, selective interpretation, wilful blindness, strategic exploitation and deliberate wrongdoing may enter at different points and coexist. The inquiry asks how the configuration enables, amplifies, conceals, constrains or exposes these actions without automatically attributing motive from harmful outcomes.

Chinese configurational thinking provides selected analytical prompts for examining sedimented form or readiness (形), relative position and connection (位／连), accumulated directional momentum (势／向), consequential openings or leverage points (机／权), and transformation (化／变). These concepts will be operationalised through the mapping method rather than treated as claims that replace established legal, administrative or systems scholarship.

## 4. Robodebt as the foundational case

Robodebt provides a documented retrospective case through which the method can be developed and tested. Official reports, judgments, testimony, administrative records and affected accounts make it possible to reconstruct how the arrangement changed over time while distinguishing documented facts from inference and hypothesis.

One provisional focus is an **evidentiary-authorisation hinge**:

1. institutional disposition and changing pressure favour a particular goal;
2. the contemplated action creates demand for evidence capable of authorising it;
3. available data and a formal operation offer a convenient bridge across an evidentiary gap;
4. rules and workflow admit the constructed output as actionable;
5. repeated use, institutional investment and defence add momentum and apparent legitimacy; and
6. the output begins to function as authority, shifting evidentiary and corrective burdens onto affected people.

The research will test this proposed hinge rather than assume it. It will examine why annual averaging became institutionally available and actionable, what alternatives were known, how different actors understood the operation, and how later actions reinforced or challenged it.

The case will also be used to identify **reversibility frontiers**. Cancelling or refunding a debt may restore a financial position without restoring lost time, health, evidence, trust or opportunity. The relevant question is therefore not simply whether review existed, but what each corrective mechanism could still observe and alter when it acted.

## 5. Design method

The project’s primary practical contribution is **Configurational Jurisdiction Inquiry**.

The method constructs provisional configurations from affected, frontline, technical, policy, legal, managerial, judicial, political and oversight positions. It compares their boundaries, visible effects, experienced burdens, actionable relations and possible interventions without assuming that one observer possesses the complete configuration.

Its principal visual instrument is a revisable **Configurational Transformation Map**. The map records:

- what has taken form;
- which operations have become available;
- where movement is amplified or attenuated;
- what information is observed, inferred, disputed, withheld or unknown;
- where stress and vulnerability are accumulating;
- what consequences remain preventable, interruptible or restorable; and
- which changes acquire enough force to affect the subsequent configuration.

Participation is meaningful when knowledge from a position revises the operative representation—for example, by changing the inquiry boundary, revealing an unrecorded burden, correcting a presumed sequence or relocating a possible intervention.

The map is designed for conditions in which information is incomplete or strategically limited. It can retain competing configurations, record confidence and contradiction, and use **blind-spot triangulation** to develop bounded hypotheses about hidden transitions by comparing what entered the arrangement, its apparent purposes and pressures, the operations available, the output produced and the effects pursued.

## 6. Prospective application

**Prospective Configurational Oversight** is the method’s initial Ombudsman-facing application.

The Ombudsman is treated as one situated participant within the configuration, not as an observer possessing a complete system view. Its complaint channels, information access, investigation triggers, timescale and routes to intervention shape what it can perceive and change.

The application will test whether configurational mapping can help an investigator:

- connect recurring complaints that appear administratively separate;
- monitor combinations of known algorithms, institutional pressures and changing goals;
- identify stress points and interfaces vulnerable to power;
- preserve competing accounts under uncertain evidence;
- distinguish warning from an intervention capable of changing the arrangement; and
- determine when escalation, suspension, evidence preservation or wider inquiry may still affect the production relation.

The work will also examine the configuration of oversight itself, including its blind spots, delays, dependencies and limited alteration powers.

## 7. Research sequence

| Stage | Activity | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Retrospective reconstruction | Verify successive Robodebt configurations from primary and authoritative sources. | Evidence-linked map, hinge analysis and reversibility findings. |
| 2. Multi-positional revision | Invite affected and institutional participants to contest and revise the reconstruction, subject to access and ethics approval. | Revised boundaries, relations, absences and competing configurations. |
| 3. Method development | Translate case findings and positional differences into the minimum viable Configurational Jurisdiction Inquiry. | Revised mapping, comparison, evidence and intervention operations. |
| 4. Scenario-based experiments | Test prospective oversight actions under incomplete, disputed and strategically limited information. | Evidence of usefulness, burdens, failure conditions and capture risks. |
| 5. Consolidation | Integrate empirical and design findings. | Bounded account of jurisdictional recession and a tested research method. |

## 8. Contribution and scope

The project aims to produce:

- an evidence-linked configurational reconstruction of Robodebt;
- a bounded account of jurisdictional recession;
- a tested Configurational Jurisdiction Inquiry method;
- an Ombudsman-facing prospective application;
- design principles for maintaining corrective reach and reversibility; and
- an account of participation as revision of the operative system representation.

The PhD is bounded to one deep foundational case and one initial institutional application. It will not attempt to explain every dimension of Robodebt, establish a comprehensive theory of institutional failure or validate a universal framework across multiple completed cases.

A live government deployment is not required for viability. The core research can be completed through documentary reconstruction, limited participant engagement and retrospective and prospective scenario-based experiments. Institutional collaboration would deepen the testing without being a precondition for completion.

## Document status

This is a short proposal prepared for PhD application and supervisory discussion. The concept, case reconstruction and design prototype remain open to empirical testing and revision.
