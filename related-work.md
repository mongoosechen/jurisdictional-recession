# Related Work

The proposed PhD project forms one bounded part of a wider independent research programme concerned with positional knowledge, authority, actionability and the conditions under which corrective responses can change a system.

The works listed below provide conceptual background or demonstrate related research capabilities. They are not proposed as separate components of the PhD.

## Democratic Configurational Intelligence

**Status:** Working paper

Democratic Configurational Intelligence investigates how configurational knowledge can be used without assuming the position of a ruler, manager or central observer. It examines how knowledge changes function when it moves between differently situated positions and how people affected by an arrangement can contribute to its representation without being treated merely as information sources.

Its relationship to the proposed PhD is methodological. It supports the multi-positional design of Configurational Jurisdiction Inquiry, including:

- positional non-equivalence;
- knowledge from non-controlling positions;
- revision of the operative representation;
- critique displacement;
- the unequal circulation of authority and knowledge; and
- democratic transposition of configurational knowledge.

## Protocol of Right-Leveling

**Status:** Method paper and worked case

The Protocol of Right-Leveling is a diagnostic method for examining whether a problem has been presented at the level where the decisive authority, constraint or framing operation occurs.

Its relationship to the proposed PhD is diagnostic. It supports questions such as:

- Who has been positioned as the decision-maker?
- Who is exposed to the consequences?
- At what level is challenge permitted?
- Does an available remedy reach the relation producing the problem?
- Has an institutional framing displaced attention from an upstream authority-producing operation?

The Protocol of Right-Leveling is developed separately and is not an additional method to be validated within the jurisdictional-recession PhD.

[View the Protocol of Right-Leveling](https://github.com/mongoosechen/Protocol-of-Right-Leveling-PRL-)
